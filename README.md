# moh-icons

SVG icons for *Medal of Honor: Allied Assault* and its two expansions.

| Original&nbsp;design | New&nbsp;design | Game | Based on |
|:-:|:-:|---|---|
| <img src="originals/mohaa.png" width="64" alt="Original Allied Assault icon"> | <img src="mohaa.svg?v=3" width="64" alt="Allied Assault icon"> | Allied Assault | The Allied star: the star-in-a-circle marking painted on Allied vehicles in WWII |
| | <img src="mohaa-transparent.svg" width="64" alt="Allied Assault icon, transparent background"> | Allied Assault (alternative) | The same star with no background, so whatever is behind it shows through the gaps. |
| <img src="originals/mohaa-spearhead.png" width="64" alt="Original Spearhead icon"> | <img src="mohaa-spearhead.svg?v=2" width="64" alt="Spearhead icon"> | Spearhead | The military chevron: three stacked like a sergeant's stripes. The chevrons are curved, like real rank insignia, instead of the original icon's straight ones. |
| <img src="originals/mohaa-breakthrough.png" width="64" alt="Original Breakthrough icon"> | <img src="mohaa-breakthrough.svg?v=3" width="64" alt="Breakthrough icon"> | Breakthrough | The US Army Special Forces patch: a dagger crossed by three lightning bolts. |

All three use the same gold (`#B8962E`). Like the Allied star, the Spearhead and Breakthrough emblems break through the gold ring where they reach it. Their backgrounds match the original icons: green `#244C31` for Spearhead and indigo `#2C2564` for Breakthrough.

The originals are the games' 32×32 icons, kept in [`originals/`](originals).
